LODESTAR CAIRN - FEATURES AND COMMANDS (Ver. 1.2.0, ALPHA)
By Wolf Reactive Systems (WRS). For the Heltec WiFi LoRa 32 V4 R2 (not the R8). CAIRN Tactical Unit (CTU) firmware.
ALPHA: tested on a PC, in a browser with practice data and by compiling the firmware; not yet run on real hardware by the authors.

WHAT CAIRN IS
A MeshCore companion radio (NOVA, plus extras) for an emergency and everyday field kit. It has only the tactical
dashboard (no pet, no shark tank). It keeps a journal of everything it hears, has a station ID, sends check-ins,
status, marks and help calls, and feeds a tablet dashboard that works with no internet. Aimed at ordinary people:
an Easy view with big buttons and a plain-language field guide.

FEATURES
- Station ID: LC- plus 8 letters/digits from the last 5 bytes of the radio's public key. Random at first boot, never
  changes, survives reboot. Not checked against any list (offline).
- I'M OK: "LC-xxxxxxxx Reporting at: <lat>,<lon>" (or "no GPS fix") to the check-in channel (default 0, Public).
- HELP: "LC-xxxxxxxx NEEDS HELP at: <lat>,<lon>", repeating every 1-60 minutes (default 5) until cancelled; always at
  full power; kept over a restart; cancelling sends an I'M OK. Dashboard asks "Send a call for help?" first and says to
  call 911 first. On the radio's OLED it needs two presses (arm, then send) so a pocket press cannot send it.
- STATUS (one tap): Need medical / water / food / shelter / transport, Evacuating, Staying put, Can help.
  Text: "LC-xxxxxxxx STATUS NEED WATER at: <lat>,<lon>".
- MARK this place: Water, Shelter, Medical aid, Hazard, Meeting point, Road blocked, Supplies.
  Text: "LC-xxxxxxxx MARK HAZARD at: <lat>,<lon>". Shown on every CAIRN dashboard map and in the report.
- LOW BATTERY: sent once at 15% on battery ("LC-xxxxxxxx LOW BATTERY 15% at: ..."); re-arms above 30% or on charge.
- Automatic I'M OK every 10-1440 minutes (not while a help call runs; twice as slow on a low battery).
- Channels: add/remove from the dashboard Chat tab or by command. Open "#name" channels (key made from the name, like
  the MeshCore app), private channels (new random key, or name + 32-hex key), show key, remove, choose which channel
  carries check-ins. Up to 40 channels; channel 0 (Public) cannot be removed.
- Standard mode: the highest transmit power the US unlicensed rule allows with your antenna (see POWER); battery
  saving lowers it below 25% battery. Disaster mode: the same legal maximum, always (even on a low battery), quiet hours
  off, screen off sooner, stale repeaters marked sooner. Both are remembered over a restart.
- Journal: every message, advert, check-in/help/status/mark sent, every boot. Saved on the radio (/cairn.log, rotated
  at 192 KB to /cairn.1) and copied to the tablet. Export CSV or JSON lines.
- Settings kept on the radio in /cairn.cfg (mode, power choice, antenna gain, automatic I'M OK, help interval, battery
  saving, low-battery message, check-in channel, help call on/off).
- OLED: CAIRN splash, CAIRN STATION page (hold = check-in), HELP page (hold to arm, hold again to send / cancel), and
  POWER page (mode, radio power, battery; hold = switch Standard/Disaster).
- Everything NOVA does still works.

DASHBOARD (Chrome 148+ on Android, or a recent Chrome/Edge on a computer; BLUETOOTH firmware build)
- Opening screen (splash) that scales to any screen (designed for 1280x800); tap to skip.
- Easy view (default): Home, People, Chat, Map, Power, Guide. Full view adds Station (radar, console), Nearby, Log.
- Home: I'M OK, I NEED HELP, status, mark, Standard/Disaster, Ready check, Who is around, Situation report.
- Alerts on every screen when another station calls for help (sound + vibration, can be switched off), with distance
  and direction, "Show on map" and "Got it". Warning when the radio battery is low.
- People: everyone who reported, help calls first, statuses, low battery, overdue, and a "needs and places" summary.
- Map: offline vector maps; red pulsing dots for help calls; orange diamonds for marked places.
- Power: radio power and settings, "How long will my power last?" calculator (power banks, tablet, solar), supply timers.
- Situation report: text summary, copy / save, and a one-line version (<=140 characters) to send to everyone.
- Guide: plain-language field guide, a one-page quick-start card, print / save as PDF, download as a web page.
- Practice mode: pretend data, including incoming help calls, so every button can be tried safely.

POWER AND THE LAW (honest summary)
- Radio: Heltec V4 (SX1262 + external amplifier). MeshCore "tx power" setting 10 gives about 22 dBm (158 mW); setting 22
  gives about 28 dBm (about 630 mW; Heltec rates the board 28 +/- 1 dBm). CAIRN uses 22 for "full" when the antenna
  allows and 10 for "saver".
- US unlicensed rule used (47 CFR 15.247(b)): up to 1 watt (30 dBm) into the antenna in the 902-928 MHz band with an antenna
  of up to 6 dBi; above 6 dBi the power must come down dB for dB. CAIRN works out the setting from the antenna gain you
  enter (default 2 dBi) with a 1 dB margin for the board's tolerance.
- There is NO higher legal power for emergencies for unlicensed radios (47 CFR 2.405 applies to licensed stations), so
  Disaster mode keeps the radio at full legal power instead of exceeding it.
- Outside the 902-928 MHz band CAIRN leaves the power alone: check your local rules.
- Not yet verified for a product you sell: the narrow channel widths used by MeshCore in the US (62.5 kHz) versus the
  500 kHz minimum 6 dB bandwidth in 15.247 for digitally modulated systems, and equipment authorization and labelling.
  Have a test lab confirm this before selling radios as licence-free.
- Battery figures from the Heltec V4.3.1 datasheet: receive about 75 mA, send about 400 mA at 22 dBm and 750 mA at 27 dBm;
  solar input 4.7-6 V; battery 3.3-4.2 V; USB charge 500 mA. The hub must supply short 750 mA bursts.

COMMANDS (type them in the dashboard Console - over USB in a browser, or over Bluetooth in the CAIRN Station tablet app - or in a serial console. Not confirmed: whether the MeshCore phone app can send them)
  cairn                          status: version, ID, mode, journal size
  cairn id                       print the station ID
  cairn ok [channel]             send I'M OK now (default: the check-in channel)
  cairn help [channel]           start the repeating help call;   cairn help off   cancel it (sends I'M OK)
  cairn beacon <1-60>            minutes between help calls
  cairn ustatus <1-8> [channel]  status: 1 Need medical, 2 Need water, 3 Need food, 4 Need shelter, 5 Need transport,
                                 6 Evacuating, 7 Staying put, 8 Can help
  cairn mark <1-7> [channel]     mark this place: 1 Water, 2 Shelter, 3 Medical aid, 4 Hazard, 5 Meeting point,
                                 6 Road blocked, 7 Supplies
  cairn mode standard|disaster   change mode ("normal" also works for standard)
  cairn power full|saver|status  power choice, or show power now and the legal limit
  cairn antenna <dBi>            antenna gain, 0-20 (default 2)
  cairn auto <10-1440>|off       automatic I'M OK
  cairn eco on|off               battery saving     cairn lowbat on|off   low-battery message
  cairn main <0-39>              channel for check-ins, help, status, marks
  cairn chans                    list channels (JSON for the dashboard)
  cairn chan add #name           join an open channel          cairn chan add <name> <32-hex key>   join a private one
  cairn chan new <name>          make a private channel with a new random key
  cairn chan key <n>             show channel n's key          cairn chan remove <n>   remove channel n (not 0)
  cairn say <ch> <text>          message to channel <ch>, 140 characters at most
  cairn dm <key12> <text>        direct message to the contact whose key starts with these 12 hex characters
  cairn config                   show all CAIRN settings
  cairn dump                     send the whole journal to the serial port
  cairn clear yes                erase the journal ON THE RADIO (the tablet's copy stays)
  cairn time <seconds>           set the radio's clock (the dashboard does this automatically)
  nova ...                       all NOVA commands

REPEATER AND COMMUNICATIONS HUB
The CAIRN radio is a companion: it sends and receives your messages, but it does not repeat other people's. The
repeating is done by the second radio in the kit (XIAO ESP32S3 running PULSAR), powered from the hub.

LIMITS
- The radio cannot see power-bank or tablet charge; the Power tab timers and calculator are estimates.
- Keep antennas apart and away from the USB hub. A mesh radio is a backup, not a replacement for 911.
- A help call can only be heard by radios in range. It does not contact emergency services.
