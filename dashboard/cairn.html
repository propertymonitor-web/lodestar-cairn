      Radio power and settings
      
        
      Connect the radio to see its settings.
      
        
      
        
      
          
      
          
      
          
      
          
      
          
      
          
      
        
      
        
      About the power limit: US rules for radios used without a licence (FCC Part 15, section 15.247) allow up to 1 watt at the antenna with an antenna up to 6 dBi, and less with a bigger antenna. There is no higher limit for emergencies for unlicensed radios, so Standard and Disaster both use the most the rules allow. The CAIRN radio works out the setting from the antenna gain you enter. Using it outside the US, or on another frequency, follow your own country's rules.

      How long will my power last?
      
        
      Fill in what you have. The answer is an estimate, not a promise: real use varies with the cold, the tablet's screen, and how often you send.
      
        
      
          
      
          
      
          
      
          
      
          
      
          
      
          
      
        
      
        
      
        
      How this is worked out: the two radios and the hub are counted at their listening draw (about 1.2 W together) plus a little extra for each message sent; a power bank gives about 80% of its printed size; solar gives about 70% of panel watts times sun hours. Datasheet figures for the Heltec V4: listening about 75 mA, sending about 400 mA at 22 dBm and 750 mA at 27 dBm (battery side); a radio sending at full power needs a hub that can supply short 750 mA peaks.
      
      
      
      
      
        
      Power banks and other supplies (a timer you set)
      
        
      The radios cannot see how full a power bank is, so this is a reminder, not a gauge. Enter how long you expect each one to last, and press the button when you start (or recharge) it.
