LODESTAR CAIRN - FLASHING AND SETUP GUIDE (Ver. 1.0.3, ALPHA)
By FEAR REACTIVE. Written for a beginner. You need a Heltec WiFi LoRa 32 V4 R2 (check the back: R2, not R8).

WHAT YOU NEED
- The Heltec V4 R2 with its antenna fitted BEFORE power (powering a LoRa radio without an antenna can damage it).
- A USB-C data cable. A computer with Chrome or Edge for flashing.
- The release files: firmware-merged.bin and dashboard.html (GitHub: propertymonitor-web/lodestar-cairn, Releases).

STEP 1 - FLASH
1. Open https://flasher.meshcore.io in Chrome or Edge.
2. Choose Custom Firmware, pick firmware-merged.bin, connect the radio, and flash.
3. The radio restarts and shows the CAIRN screen.
(Updating later: you can flash firmware-merged.bin again.)

STEP 2 - FIRST SETTINGS (MeshCore app, Bluetooth)
1. Pair the radio in the MeshCore app. Set your name and the radio settings for your region
   (USA example: 910.525 MHz, 62.5 kHz, SF 7, CR 5; check your local mesh).
2. In the command box type:  cairn   You should see "LODESTAR CAIRN 1.0.3  ID LC-xxxxxxxx". Write the ID down.
3. Reboot and type cairn again: the ID must be the same.

STEP 3 - THE DASHBOARD
Online (before the field):
1. Open the dashboard page (GitHub Pages address, or open dashboard.html from the release) in Chrome 148+ on the tablet.
2. Add it to the Home screen. Close it, turn Wi-Fi off, open it again: it must still open.
3. Map tab: pick your region, Download, then Load a map file. This lets the map work with no internet.
In the field (no internet):
1. Radio into the hub, hub into the tablet. Open the dashboard, press Connect radio, choose the radio.
2. Status says Live and your ID shows at the top. The radio clock is set automatically.

STEP 4 - THE REPEATER
Flash the XIAO ESP32S3 with LODESTAR PULSAR (see the PULSAR zip). Power it from the hub only. It does not connect to the tablet.
Keep its antenna at least an arm's length from the CAIRN radio's antenna.

STEP 5 - TEST
Follow CAIRN-TEST-CHECKLIST.txt before relying on the kit.

TROUBLESHOOTING
- Dashboard will not connect: use Chrome 148+; unplug and replug; close other apps using USB; make sure it is the BLUETOOTH build.
- No repeater heard: antenna on, go outside and higher, wait 10 minutes for adverts.
- ID changed after an update: only if the radio's identity was erased.
